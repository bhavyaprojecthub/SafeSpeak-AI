Building Safer Digital Communities with AI

SafeSpeak is an AI-powered hate speech detection platform designed to help students, educators, researchers, and online communities identify harmful language in real time. By leveraging machine learning and natural language processing, SafeSpeak promotes healthier conversations and more inclusive digital spaces.

How SafeSpeak Works

01

Enter your text

Paste any sentence, comment, social media post, or message into the text box for analysis.

02

BERT Analyzes the Content

A pretrained BERT model processes the text, understands its context, and identifies linguistic patterns associated with hateful or non-hateful language.

03

Instant Prediction

SafeSpeak classifies the input as Hate Speech or Non-Hate Speech and displays the prediction confidence through an easy-to-read confidence bar.

04

Interpret the Results

Use the insights for research, classroom discussions, or platform moderation — the choice is yours.
