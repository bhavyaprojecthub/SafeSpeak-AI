How SafeSpeak Works

A fine-tuned BERT classifier reads every message end to end, weighing context rather than matching keywords, and returns a prediction in seconds. Here's what happens between your keystroke and the result.

The Prediction Pipeline

Six steps turn raw text into a hate speech / non-hate speech verdict.

User Input

You type or paste a sentence, comment, or message into the text box on the demo page.

BERT Tokenizer

The text is split into subword tokens and converted into input IDs the model can read, with padding and attention masks added.

Fine-tuned BERT Model

A pretrained bert-base-uncased model, fine-tuned on labeled hate speech data, processes the tokens through its transformer layers.

Output Logits

The model's final layer produces raw, unnormalized scores, one logit per class, representing its internal confidence for each label.

torch.argmax()

The class with the highest logit is selected directly, no probability conversion needed, giving a fast, deterministic decision.

Prediction & Display

The result, Hate Speech or Non-Hate Speech, is sent back to the browser and rendered instantly with a confidence bar.

Why BERT?

Language is rarely just a list of bad words. BERT was built to understand it the way people actually write.

Bidirectional Understanding

BERT reads a sentence in both directions at once, so it understands a word using everything around it, not just what came before.

Context Awareness

The same word can be harmless or harmful depending on context. BERT weighs the full sentence, not isolated words, before deciding.

Transformer Architecture

Self-attention lets the model relate every word to every other word directly, capturing nuance that older models miss.

Beyond Keyword Matching

Traditional filters flag words in isolation and miss sarcasm, coded language, or context. BERT evaluates meaning, not string matches.
